About

What we do, why we do it, and how it helps.

One World Relief supports people through clean water, orphan care, feeding, zakat-based support, and urgent humanitarian outreach.

What We Do

We raise support for projects that meet real needs with clear, practical outcomes.

Why It Matters

Small donations become direct help when they are organized around real people and real needs.

How We Work

We collect, organize, share updates, and keep donors connected to visible impact.
