What can you deduct?

Debt deductions vary by scholarly approach. Choose the treatment you follow.

What about mortgages and future bills?

Deduction rules differ. Some approaches allow only amounts currently payable; others allow qualifying instalments over the coming year. A future expense is not automatically a deductible debt. NZF excludes interest charges from loan deductions. Use the eligible principal and other obligations approved under your guidance.

National Zakat Foundation: debt guidance ↗

Already paid some Zakat?

No double deduction.

Debts reduce your zakatable wealth. Zakat already paid is deducted from the Zakat amount afterwards.

Help & sources

These are short explanations of the sources, not quotations or a claim that every scholarly detail is the same.

SAHIH AL-BUKHARI · 1454

Why 2.5%?

The report of Abu Bakr’s Zakat instructions specifies one fortieth for silver. One fortieth equals 2.5%.

Read the hadith in English ↗
SUNAN ABI DAWUD · 1573

Why a Zakat year?

The narration links the gold and silver amounts to a year passing. Sunnah.com lists its grading as Sahih (Al-Albani).

Read the narration and grading ↗
QUR’AN · 9:60

Who can receive it?

The Qur’an specifies eight recipient categories. A good cause alone does not establish eligibility for Zakat.

Common questions

This calculator covers common Zakat al-mal assets. Specialist cases deserve specialist advice.

Explore the NZF knowledge bank ↗
Do I include jewellery I wear?

Scholars differ about personal-use gold and silver jewellery. Include or exclude it according to the guidance you follow. When included, use the metal’s value, not a jewellery shop’s replacement price or the value of stones.

Islamic Relief: gold, silver and jewellery ↗
How do I value shares and retirement accounts?

Trading investments may be assessed at their market value; long-term holdings can use their underlying zakatable assets. Pension and retirement rules depend on access, ownership and investments. Enter the zakatable portion determined under your guidance, rather than automatically using every account’s full value.

NZF: valuing share investments ↗
Fiqh Council of North America: retirement funds ↗
What if my wealth drops during the year?

Scholarly approaches differ on changes during the hawl. This calculator asks whether your year has completed under your guidance; it does not decide that from a single day’s balance. Choose “I am not sure” for a planning estimate and confirm the timing with a qualified scholar.

Islamic Relief: timing and thresholds ↗
Why is the solar-year rate different?

The default is 2.5% for a Hijri year. NZF explains a 2.577% rate for the longer solar year, citing AAOIFI guidance, while recommending the lunar calendar.

Read the solar-year guidance ↗
Is Zakat al-Fitr included?

No. Zakat al-Fitr is different. This calculator covers Zakat al-mal only; it does not calculate Zakat al-Fitr, agricultural produce, livestock, minerals or other categories with different rules.

Is my financial information saved?

One World Relief does not save or send your asset and debt entries. They are held in this page while you use it. Printing or saving a PDF is your choice; store that copy somewhere private.
