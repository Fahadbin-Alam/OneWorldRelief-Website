A fresh start for a father's business.

One World Relief delivered $600 in direct livelihood support for a father in Bangladesh working to restart his small vegetable business after losing income.

Overview

What this case funded

The recipient is a father of two who had worked as a vegetable seller before losing his business income. The support was given so he could restart earning, cover food needs, and keep his children moving forward in school.

Personal identity documents and home address details are kept off the public website. The donor-facing page includes the case summary, delivery photos, and video proof that can be reviewed without leaving One World Relief.
