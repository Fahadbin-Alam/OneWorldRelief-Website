The Story

Helping finish a place of prayer.

Rows of arched windows, a green-and-white facade, and bamboo scaffolding across the front: the latest photos give you a closer look at the mosque behind this project. The wider views show the building as a whole, while the close-ups bring its upper facade into focus.

One World Relief's planned contribution is tile work to help finish the mosque. For us, the purpose is to support a space that can bring people together for prayer. This case focuses on one practical part of that larger effort, with the exact areas and tile work still to be confirmed.

What comes next: We will share the agreed tile-work plan, budget, and timing as those details become available. The photos and video below let you follow the site alongside those updates and see the place this project is intended to support.
