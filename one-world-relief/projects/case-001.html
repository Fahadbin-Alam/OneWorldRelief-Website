Case 001 · Orphan Support

Hafiz student support after the loss of his father.

One World Relief delivered $600 in direct support for a young Hafiz student in Bangladesh. This page keeps the case summary, proof photos, and field videos together for donor review.

Overview

What this case funded

The support helped a young student continue his Hafiz studies and covered essential needs after his father passed away. The case was handled as a direct support project, with identifying details kept limited on the public website for privacy.

Donors can review the photos and videos below without leaving the One World Relief website. Future updates for this case should be added here with dates, amount confirmations, and any safe follow-up proof.
