A safer elder-care home.

We are planning renovation support for a home serving older adults without reliable family support. We want to help make the place they call home safer, more comfortable, and easier to live in with dignity.

The Story

Improving the place they call home.

For older adults without reliable family support, the comfort and condition of their home matter every day. One World Relief is planning renovation support with that daily life in mind: a place to rest, move between rooms, and spend time with others in comfort.

The three field videos take you through that setting. You can see the exterior walls and exposed roof framing, look inside rooms, and follow the covered walkway. Sharing these views gives you a starting point for understanding the proposed renovation and following the work as the plan takes shape.

What we are working toward: A safer, more comfortable living environment that respects residents' dignity. The building assessment will guide which repairs are included. We will share the confirmed repair plan, budget, and schedule here, followed by further updates as the project develops.
