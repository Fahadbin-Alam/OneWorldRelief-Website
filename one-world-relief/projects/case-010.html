Overview

What is planned

This coming-soon project is intended to help renovate a residential home serving vulnerable older adults who are living without reliable family support. The goal is a safer, more comfortable, and more dignified place to live.

A building assessment is still required before specific repairs can be named. Confirmed needs, costs, and approved project media will be published after verification.
