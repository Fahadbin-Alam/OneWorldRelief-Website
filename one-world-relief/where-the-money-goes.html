How a gift moves

Four steps, all on the record

  1. 1

    You give through Stripe

    Checkout is handled by Stripe. Your card details never touch our site, and your receipt is emailed when the payment succeeds.

  2. 2

    It is logged to the cause you chose

    Each gift is recorded with its cause. If a specific need is fully funded or cannot go ahead, the gift goes to the closest eligible need in the same program.

  3. 3

    Aid is delivered in the field

    Support is delivered for a specific case, such as a student's school year, a family's business, or a mosque's fans and tiles.

  4. 4

    The case is published

    Each case gets a public page with its cost, status, and field photos or video. Donor names and private documents stay off the website.
