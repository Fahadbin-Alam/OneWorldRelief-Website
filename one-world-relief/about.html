About

Direct help in Bangladesh, with proof for every case.

One World Relief is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit. We fund specific cases in Bangladesh, from keeping orphans in school to flood relief and mosque repairs, and publish photos and videos once the help is delivered.

How every case works

From a real need to published proof.

  1. 1

    A specific need

    Every case is a real person, family or community in Bangladesh, such as an orphan who needs school fees or families hit by floods.

  2. 2

    Help delivered directly

    Our field team delivers the support to the case, and the amount delivered is recorded on its page.

  3. 3

    Proof published

    Photos and videos go on the case’s own page, while private and identifying details stay off the website.
