Donation Details

Select or enter a donation amount
Visa Mastercard Stripe

You will enter your card on Stripe's secure checkout page. One World Relief never sees or stores your card number.

What your donation supports

  • $10 helps fund a basic family support need.
  • $25 supports essentials for a child or household.
  • $50 helps fund part of a relief or feeding campaign.
  • $100 contributes toward larger community projects like wells.
Secure checkout

After you continue, you are sent to Stripe's hosted checkout page to pay. You can cancel there at any time.

Where your money goes

Your gift is recorded with the cause you choose, and completed work is posted with the amount given on the Projects page.

Your information

Your name, email, amount and chosen cause are shared with Stripe to process the payment and kept in our donation records. Questions about a gift: Oneworldrelief.fma@gmail.com.
