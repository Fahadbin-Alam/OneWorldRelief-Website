Common questions

How do I donate?

Go to the donate page, choose the cause you want to support, and pay on Stripe's secure checkout page.

Can I follow project updates?

Yes. The projects page is where wells, orphan support updates, and relief campaigns are posted.

Is my card information safe?

Payments are processed by Stripe. One World Relief never sees or stores your card number.

Who should I contact for questions?

Email the contact address on this page for donation, partnership, or update questions.
